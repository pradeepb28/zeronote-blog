Paste a link to strip tracking and affiliate junk. Copy the cleaned URL. Nothing is uploaded.

## How to use this link tracker remover

1. Paste a full http(s) link. The cleaned URL updates as you type.
2. Check the note for which fields were removed.
3. Click Copy. If you need to send the link privately, use a [self-destructing note](https://zeronote.app).

Short links are not expanded. If you only have a bit.ly or t.co URL, open it first, then paste the destination.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does this tracker remover upload my link?

No. The link is cleaned in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

### Does it expand short links?

No. Shorteners such as bit.ly or t.co are not expanded. Paste the final http(s) link.

### Will the page still work after cleaning?

Unknown query keys are kept so search, product, and video parameters stay. Only known tracking and affiliate fields are removed.

### Is a screenshot or QR of the original link clean?

No. The trackers are in the URL. Clean the link here, then share or encode that result.

### How should I share the cleaned link?

Copy it, then send it in a [note that can disappear](/posts/share-private-info-securely/) if it should not sit in chat or email history.
