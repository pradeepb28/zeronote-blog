Photos often store where they were taken, when, and which phone or camera was used. Clean that metadata here before you share the file.

## How to use this EXIF cleaner

1. Choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP, or drop it on the dashed box. Nothing is uploaded.
2. Review the identifying fields. Everything shown is checked to remove.
3. Uncheck a field only if you want to keep it.
4. Click Remove selected, then Download. You get a copy; the original on your disk is not overwritten.

Orientation and the ICC color profile stay in the file so the photo does not flip or shift color.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does this EXIF cleaner upload my photo?

No. The file stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

### Does cleaning change the picture itself?

No. Only metadata is rewritten. The image pixels are not recompressed or resized.

### Which photo formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP. iPhone HEIC photos are not supported; export as JPEG and try again.

### What metadata is removed?

Fields that can identify where, when, and which device was used, plus author, contact, and document packets when present. Orientation and the color profile are kept so the photo does not flip or change color.

### How should I share the cleaned photo?

Download the copy, then send that file. Do not send the original if it still has location or device data.
